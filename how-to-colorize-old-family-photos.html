How to colorize old family photos

Updated 2026-11-06 · Colorize Old · colorizeold.com

Old family prints often sit in a shoebox as black-and-white or faded scans. AI colorization can make relatives easier to “meet” in color — as long as you treat the result as an interpretation, not a recovered Kodachrome original.

1. Digitize the print

  • Use even light or a flatbed; avoid glare.
  • Save a master file (JPEG/PNG; longest side ≥ 1200 px when possible).
  • Keep the original B&W forever alongside any color version.

2. Optional light cleanup

Crop, straighten, gentle contrast. Skip heavy beauty filters before colorizing.
