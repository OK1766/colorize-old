Restore old family photos — start with honest color

Updated 2026-11-06 · Colorize Old · colorizeold.com

Old family prints fade, yellow, or stay black-and-white in a shoebox. “Restore” here means two practical steps: digitize carefully, then add historically plausible color so relatives are easier to see — without pretending the AI recovered the original Kodachrome.

1. Digitize once, keep the master

Even light or a flatbed. Save a master JPEG/PNG (longest side ≥ 1200 px when you can). Keep the original scan forever beside any color version.
